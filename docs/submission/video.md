# Demo video storyboard — Tell

- **Target length: 2:15** (the hard limit is under 3:00; "Judges are not required to watch beyond three minutes").
- **Rule to follow:** "show real gameplay that honestly represents the experience. Lead with the story and what makes your project distinct. Don't lean on AI-generated video to carry the pitch." (<https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules>, §6). All gameplay must be real headset capture on Quest 3 in passthrough. No AI-generated footage, no mock-ups.
- **Story in one line:** *They read your real hands, so you can lie with them.*
- **Shoot plan:** one afternoon, solo, at your own desk, plus one external phone shot. Edit the next day.
- **Must be done before 11/10.** Upload to YouTube (public or unlisted is not enough: the rules say "publicly visible").

### Optional voice-over (only if it does not crowd the game audio)

The game audio carries the story, and the characters' lines are the best VO. If you add a narrator, record it separately (phone, close mic, quiet room) and keep it to these three lines. Each must also appear as a subtitle:

- 0:20 — "This is Tell. Poker on your real desk, with your bare hands."
- 1:00 — "You read them…" / 1:25 — "…and they read you."
- 2:08 — "Read them. Fool them."

## What must be captured in the headset (passthrough)

Record each beat as its own take (MQDH caps a recording at three minutes). Do several takes and keep the best.

1. **Hook (shots 1–5):** wrist menu → **Fool Rocco** (only in a dev-tools build since 2026-10-15: `TELL_DEV_TOOLS=1 tools/build-quest.sh`; that build also puts the gesture recorder panel and hand debug readout on the table, so keep them out of frame or crop). Play hand 1 with an obvious slow reach so Rocco says the "slow reach" line. In hand 2, reach slowly on purpose (wait at least the slow-reach time), bet, let Rocco call, win the showdown, and capture "You played me!" plus the reveal card. If hand 1 produces a different read (for example "You looked at your cards twice"), that also works: use whichever read is clearest, and change the on-screen text only if needed.
2. **Cold launch:** quit the app, relaunch, and capture the table snapping to the desk and the characters appearing (shot 8).
3. **Gesture takes (shots 9–13):** one clean take per gesture, with your hands well lit and centered in view. Do the tap check with a visible physical tap.
4. **Gaze takes (14–15):** hold your head still on Pip, then on Vex, for about 3 s each.
5. **Showdown reveal (16)** in a normal game.
6. **Live read (17–19):** a normal game where you reach and pull back on your turn. "Not this time" needs a second fake against Rocco (repeat Fool Rocco, or keep faking in a normal game).
7. **Voice (20):** turn Voice chat on (Settings → Voice chat → I agree) on a good network. Ask two or three short questions and keep the best answer.
8. **Tutorial (21):** reset progress or start with a fresh install, then capture the first demonstration step and the end-of-game screen.
9. **Clean plate:** 10 s of the empty desk without the app (for a transition, optional).

Logs that help you find the takes: `adb logcat -s Unity | grep -E "\[FoolRocco\]|\[LiveTalk\]|\[Read\]|\[Voice\]"`.

## Capture settings for Quest 3

**Primary: Meta Quest Developer Hub (MQDH) Record** (<https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/ts-mqdh-media/>):

1. Connect the headset (USB) and open MQDH → **Device Manager** → **Device Actions** → gear icon next to **Record**.
2. **Resolution: 1080p @ 36fps (16:9)**. **Bitrate: 40 Mbps**. **Eye view: Single Eye** (monoscopic, the one that lists audio). Save.
3. Record with **Record** or Ctrl+Shift+R. The limit is three minutes per clip. Files on macOS go to `~/Library/Application Support/odh/captures`.
4. **Audio:** Meta's page is inconsistent about whether audio is recorded. **Test first:** record 10 s with a character speaking and check that game audio is present. If there is no audio, record the game audio separately (see below).

**Alternative: higher-quality Cast recording** (same page): record from the MQDH Cast window with target frame rate **60 FPS**, **Pause When Recording** on, and **MAX** capture format. This needs a lot of disk space (about 600 MB per minute).

**Fallback: the in-headset camera** (Meta button → Camera → Record video, with "Record microphone" on). This records game audio and your voice, but it defaults to a square 1:1 frame at 30 fps, so crop to 16:9 in the edit and keep the action centered while you play.

**Microphone / your voice (shot 20):**

- The app itself uses the microphone for voice chat. First check that the system recorder can capture mic audio at the same time. If it can't, also record the room with your phone placed on the desk, then sync your voice to the headset video using the chip and tap sounds.
- Record the optional narrator VO separately, as noted above.

**Before you start:**

- Charge to 100%, clean the lenses, and light the desk evenly (hand tracking needs light; avoid a bright window behind you).
- Clear the desk: plain surface, no branded items.
- Turn on **Do Not Disturb** so no system notifications appear in the captures.
- Check the voice relay: `curl https://vr-poker-api.elebymax.com/health`.
- Keep your head movement slow and steady. Captures are taken from one eye and fast head turns look jarring on a flat screen.

## Editing notes

- **Edit to 16:9 at 1920×1080, 30 fps** (MQDH captures at 36 fps; conform to 30 or keep 36 consistently). Export H.264 at a high bitrate, AAC audio.
- **The first 20 s carry the video.** Use hard cuts, no logo intro, and get Rocco's voice in within the first second. Keep the title card short (shot 6, about 3 s).
- **Subtitles:** burn in every spoken line (characters and your own voice) in the same style as the in-game subtitles. Many judges watch muted.
- **Music:** one royalty-free track whose license you can document. Duck it under character lines (−12 dB). No popular music.
- **Honesty:** no speed ramps that misrepresent latency in the voice shot; trim only the silence before you speak. Don't composite UI that does not exist in the app.
- **No AI-generated video or imagery.** A static logo you made is fine.
- **Speed:** use cut-downs, not time-lapse, to show gestures. Each gesture should get 3–5 s.
- **Final check:** total length 2:10–2:20. Watch it once with the sound off (the subtitles must carry the story) and once on a phone (the text must be readable).
- **Upload:** YouTube, **Public**, title "Tell — Mixed Reality Poker with Bare Hands (Meta Quest 3)". Paste the Devpost tagline into the YouTube description, then put the link in the Devpost form.
- **Reuse:** the store trailer has to be 30 s – 2 min, MP4/H.264/AAC, 1080p minimum (<https://developers.meta.com/horizon/resources/asset-guidelines/>). Cut a 60–90 s version from the same edit.
